MTN MoMo Registration

Open your mobile money wallet so you can send and receive money.

  1. Gather your Ghana Card and the phone with your MTN SIM card. The SIM must be registered in your name.
  2. Visit a MoMo Agent or an MTN service centre near you. Look for the yellow MoMo sign.
  3. Give the Agent your Ghana Card. The Agent will enter your details and open your wallet.
  4. Dial the MoMo short code on your phone to open your wallet menu. Dial *170#
  5. Follow the prompts to create your secret MoMo PIN. Choose numbers only you will remember. Never share your PIN with anyone.
  6. Wait for the confirmation SMS from MTN. Your wallet is now ready to use.

NHIS Renewal by Phone

Renew your National Health Insurance membership with a short code.

  1. Find your NHIS membership number. It is printed on your NHIS card.
  2. Make sure your MoMo wallet has enough money to pay the premium.
  3. Dial the NHIS short code to open the renewal menu. Dial *929#
  4. Choose the option for renewing your membership, then enter your NHIS membership number when asked.
  5. Confirm the amount shown, then enter your MoMo PIN to pay.
  6. Wait for the confirmation SMS. Keep it as proof of payment until your card is updated.

Buy ECG Prepaid Credit

Top up your electricity meter using your Mobile Money wallet.

  1. Find the meter number on the front of your prepaid electricity meter.
  2. Dial the MoMo short code to open your wallet menu. Dial *170#
  3. Choose the option to pay a bill, then choose ECG prepaid from the list.
  4. Enter your meter number carefully, then enter the amount you want to buy.
  5. Enter your MoMo PIN to confirm payment.
  6. You will receive an SMS with a token, which is a long number. Type this token into your meter keypad and press the enter key.

What the words mean

Agent
A trained person, usually at a small shop or kiosk, who helps you deposit, withdraw or register.
Dial
To type a number into your phone and press the green call button.
ECG
The Electricity Company of Ghana, which supplies power to homes.
Ghana Card
The national identity card issued by the National Identification Authority.
Membership number
The number on your NHIS card that identifies you in the system.
Meter number
The number printed on your electricity meter that identifies it. ECG needs it to send credit to the right meter.
MoMo
Short for Mobile Money. It is like a bank account that lives on your phone.
NHIS
The National Health Insurance Scheme. It helps you pay for hospital care.
PIN
A secret number you choose to protect your money. Never tell it to anyone.
Premium
The amount of money you pay to keep your insurance active.
Prepaid
You pay first, then use. For electricity, you buy credit before you use the power.
Short code
A short number starting with a star and ending with a hash, like *170#. You dial it like a phone number to open a menu.
SMS
A text message sent to your phone.
Token
A long number sent by SMS. You type it into your electricity meter to load credit.
USSD
The technology behind short codes. It shows you a menu on your phone screen without needing internet.
Wallet
The place on your phone where your Mobile Money is kept.
